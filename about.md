<section class="page-hero"><div class="page-width page-hero__inner"><p class="eyebrow">Our team</p><h1>Research and engineering, together in Prague</h1><p>We are an international team of more than 100 researchers, engineers, and data scientists building machine-learning systems for Cisco Security.</p></div></section>

<section class="section"><div class="page-width content-grid"><div class="content-aside"><p class="eyebrow">Who we are</p><h2>One Prague community, broad security impact</h2></div><div class="prose"><p>Our team grew from Cognitive Security, a Czech Technical University spin-off acquired by Cisco in 2013. Today, Prague data scientists contribute across Cisco XDR, Cisco Talos, Cisco Email Threat Defense, and Splunk Security while sharing a strong local research and engineering culture.</p><p>Our focus is complex threat detection. We analyze telemetry from millions of devices to identify suspicious behavior, combining machine learning and artificial intelligence with deep security expertise.</p><p>We own the full research and development journey: shaping an idea, creating prototypes, designing and evaluating models on very large datasets, and delivering scalable, efficient, automated machine-learning pipelines into production.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="section section--soft"><div class="page-width"><div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">What we contribute</p><h2>Applied AI across the detection lifecycle</h2><p class="section-heading__lead">Our teams work across data foundations, detection models, threat intelligence, and the analyst experience—turning research into dependable security capabilities.</p></div><div class="success-grid">
<article class="success-card"><span>Data foundations</span><h3>Security telemetry at scale</h3><p>We build high-throughput ingestion and streaming foundations, normalize diverse security data, and align it with open standards such as OCSF.</p></article>
<article class="success-card"><span>Behavior analytics</span><h3>Finding meaningful anomalies</h3><p>Unsupervised learning, graph methods, and behavioral models help distinguish suspicious activity across networks and endpoints.</p></article>
<article class="success-card"><span>Email security</span><h3>Language models for focused tasks</h3><p>Specialized, efficient language models help detect phishing intent, deceptive context, obfuscation, and other evasive techniques in email.</p></article>
<article class="success-card"><span>Threat intelligence</span><h3>Learning in both directions</h3><p>Automated feedback loops connect applied detections with global threat intelligence so research and operational knowledge continuously reinforce one another.</p></article>
<article class="success-card"><span>Investigation</span><h3>Evidence analysts can act on</h3><p>We combine model outputs and entity relationships into coherent context that helps security teams understand and investigate incidents.</p></article>
<article class="success-card"><span>Production engineering</span><h3>Research built to operate</h3><p>Scalable data pipelines, automated evaluation, and disciplined engineering move promising ideas into reliable production systems.</p></article>
</div></div></section>

<section class="section"><div class="page-width"><div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">Beyond our products</p><h2>Connected to the research community</h2></div><div class="capability-grid"><article class="capability-card"><span class="capability-card__number">01</span><h3>University partnerships</h3><p>We work with the Czech Technical University, Charles University, and other research institutions in the Czech Republic and internationally.</p></article><article class="capability-card"><span class="capability-card__number">02</span><h3>Public research</h3><p>Our researchers contribute peer-reviewed work to respected security and machine-learning venues and add inventions to Cisco’s patent portfolio.</p></article><article class="capability-card"><span class="capability-card__number">03</span><h3>Data Science Summit</h3><p>Each year we bring together data scientists and AI experts from across Cisco to review, demonstrate, and share their work.</p></article></div></div></section>

<section class="section section--soft"><div class="page-width content-grid"><div class="content-aside"><p class="eyebrow">Where we are</p><h2>In the heart of Prague</h2></div><div class="prose"><p>Our office is in Charles Square Center, across the street from the historic Czech Technical University building and just a few steps from the Vltava River. The windows look toward Prague Castle.</p><p>Co-location gives specialists from different parts of Cisco and Splunk a high-bandwidth environment for exchanging ideas, sharing engineering patterns, and collaborating on research. Reading groups, technical sessions, and joint projects help knowledge travel between teams.</p><p>The <a href="http://alesjungmann.cz/project/cisco/">high-tech office</a> spans two floors and includes a bouldering room, a massage chair, and kitchens stocked with good coffee and tea. The building itself brings a supermarket, stationery shop, café, bank, florist, post office, organic food shop, and gym under one roof.</p><p>Curious about the neighborhood? Read more about the <a href="https://www.karlakshopping.cz/en/about/">history of Charles Square</a>, or <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">find our address and contacts</a>.</p></div></div></section>
