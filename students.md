<section class="section"><div class="page-width content-grid"><div class="content-aside"><p class="eyebrow">Working with us</p><h2>A demanding start to your career</h2></div><div class="prose"><p>Our roots are academic: the team began at the Czech Technical University as the Cognitive Security startup COSE before Cisco acquired it in 2013. We have continued to work closely with universities and research institutions ever since.</p><p>Each year we propose MSc and BSc thesis topics. Collaboration often starts as a student project and, when it works well for everyone, continues in a part-time Cisco role. Many former students are now full-time colleagues.</p><p>This route is for students who want a genuinely challenging problem rather than the quickest possible thesis. Topics are starting points, not fixed specifications; we will help shape the work around your interests and skills.</p><p>Programming and data-processing experience provide a strong foundation. Knowledge of AI, machine learning, or data science helps, but curiosity, enthusiasm, and a willingness to learn matter just as much.</p></div></div></section>

<div class="topic-heading" id="software-engineering"><p class="eyebrow">Software engineering</p><h2>Build the systems around the science</h2></div>
<article class="thesis-card" id="evaluation-tool"><span>Software engineering</span><h3>Tool for Assisted Evaluation of Security Engine’s Efficacy</h3><p>Collaborate with data scientists to create a tool that improves how they evaluate the results produced by a security engine.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="cloud-infrastructure"><span>Software engineering</span><h3>Improving Cloud Infrastructure</h3><p>Work with engineers to improve AWS services, making deployed systems faster and more efficient. Enhance deployment scripts and investigate useful AWS services.</p><p><strong>Typical tools:</strong> Terraform, Python, Kotlin, with possible use of Scala, Java, and Ansible. Familiarity with Jupyter, Dask, PyTorch, Spark, or Hadoop is useful.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<div class="topic-heading" id="research-topics"><p class="eyebrow">Research</p><h2>Explore new approaches to security</h2></div>
<article class="thesis-card" id="regexp-generation"><span>Machine learning</span><h3>Regexp Generation from Examples</h3><p>Security systems often use regular expressions to identify URI patterns associated with a threat. Develop an algorithm that generates useful expressions from examples, then evaluate it on existing data.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="graph-learning"><span>Graph machine learning</span><h3>Learning from Multi-Modal Data Using Graph-Based Approaches</h3><p>Investigate learning and inference on graphs whose nodes represent endpoints, servers, binaries, WHOIS records, Tor nodes, certificate authorities, autonomous systems, and other entities. Potential directions include encoding neighborhoods, building classifiers that operate directly on graphs or subgraphs, incorporating time-series telemetry, or enriching nodes and edges with new labels.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="asset-detection"><span>Network intelligence</span><h3>Network Asset Detection and Service Description</h3><p>Discover network assets from destination IPs, URLs, command-line arguments, and other signals—even when telemetry captures only part of the communication—and describe the services running on each asset.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="device-actions"><span>Anomaly detection</span><h3>Unsupervised Identification of Suspicious Device Actions</h3><p>Move beyond isolated events such as a burst of traffic to one port. Model richer device behavior—when it is active, the protocols it uses, and the applications it runs—then detect anomalies as those high-level patterns evolve. The work includes designing new behavioral features that improve detection precision.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="regular-languages"><span>Explainable AI</span><h3>Induction of Regular Languages for Behavioral Malware Descriptions</h3><p>Represent observations from multiple IDS and IPS systems as a language describing malicious activity. Propose a data-driven method that learns explainable malware descriptions from these atomic observations.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="alert-recommendations"><span>Recommendation systems</span><h3>Recommendation System for Security Alerts</h3><p>Security teams can investigate only a portion of their alerts, and each team prioritizes differently according to severity, expertise, environment, and technology. Develop a recommendation system using customer feedback, and explore prioritization by industry or analyst experience.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="self-supervised"><span>Machine learning</span><h3>Self-Supervised Learning in Cybersecurity</h3><p>Study current self-supervised methods and adapt promising approaches to a cybersecurity problem, accounting for the unusual and often inconvenient characteristics of security data.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
<article class="thesis-card" id="detector-ensemble"><span>Semi-supervised learning</span><h3>Learning Parameters of Detector Ensembles from Limited Labels</h3><p>A security system may combine many specialized detectors into one verdict. Explore how to automate the aggregation process when labeled examples are scarce, using the much larger volume of unlabeled data to tune the ensemble.</p><a href="#topics">Back to topic index ↑</a></article>
